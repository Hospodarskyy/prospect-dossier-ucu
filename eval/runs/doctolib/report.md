# Doctolib

## Snapshot
- Doctolib provides an online healthcare platform for booking appointments and practice-management software for practitioners (https://multiples.vc/private-comps/doctolib)
- Doctolib's corporate headquarters is in Levallois-Perret, France (https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/62992-09)
- Doctolib has approximately 3,000 employees as of 2026 (https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/62992-09)
- Key investors in Doctolib include Bpifrance, Kerala Ventures, Accel, and Eurazeo (https://multiples.vc/private-comps/doctolib)

## Recent news
- Doctolib arranged a planned minority investment from NVIDIA to support its development of healthcare AI tools (date: 2026-10-14) (https://www.healthcapital.de/en/news/article/doctolib-accelerates-in-healthcare-ai-with-a-new-planned-investment-from-nvidia/)

## Tech signals
- Doctolib's broader technology environment spans Java, Python, and Swift (https://builtin.com/job/fullstack-software-engineer-react-kotlin-ruby-rails-x-f-m/10683258)
