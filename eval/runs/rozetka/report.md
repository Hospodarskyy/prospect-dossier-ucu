# Rozetka

## Snapshot
- Rozetka is one of the largest e-commerce companies in Ukraine, founded in 2005 in Kyiv by Vladyslav and Iryna Chechotkin. (https://forbes.ua/news/rozetka-skorochue-chastinu-shtatu-pislya-znishchennya-skladu-v-brovarakh-i-zminyue-biznes-model-12082026-41164)
- Investment fund Horizon Capital became a co-owner of Rozetka in 2015, and the company has over 540 stores in more than 165 Ukrainian cities with over 5,000 employees. (https://forbes.ua/news/rozetka-skorochue-chastinu-shtatu-pislya-znishchennya-skladu-v-brovarakh-i-zminyue-biznes-model-12082026-41164)
- Rozetka merged with marketplace operator EVO to expand its product range, IT infrastructure, and logistics capabilities. (https://horizoncapital.com.ua/news-post/rozetka-and-evo-merge-to-create-ukraines-e-commerce-champion/)

## Recent news
- Rozetka accelerated the restructuring of its business model to reduce self-managed retail sales and transition toward a decentralized network of smaller fulfillment hubs. (date: 2026-11-12) (https://dev.ua/news/chechotkina-pro-sklady-dlia-rozetka-1794498664)
- Commercial property owners in Ukraine have begun refusing to rent warehouse spaces to Rozetka due to risks of missile strikes. (date: 2026-11-12) (https://dev.ua/news/chechotkina-pro-sklady-dlia-rozetka-1794498664)
- Rozetka submitted proposals to Ukrainian authorities for post-strike tax deferrals and relief, which were rejected by the Ministry of Finance. (date: 2026-10-08) (https://dev.ua/en/news/minfin-ne-pidtrymav-propozytsiiu-rozetky-1791474499)

## Tech signals
- Rozetka's software platform is engineered around a microservices architecture supporting website features, automated warehouses, parcel lockers, and financial systems. (https://jobs.dou.ua/companies/rozetka-ua-internet-supermarket/vacancies/363572/)
- The core backend tech stack utilized by Rozetka includes PHP, Yii2, Redis, RabbitMQ, Docker, PostgreSQL, and Elasticsearch. (https://jobs.dou.ua/companies/rozetka-ua-internet-supermarket/vacancies/363572/)
- Rozetka advertises open technology roles, including backend engineering openings for PHP developers. (https://jobs.dou.ua/companies/rozetka-ua-internet-supermarket/vacancies/363572/)
